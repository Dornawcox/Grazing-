🌾 Talk to the Farm — Grazing Intake System

Conversational site assessment for regenerative grazing operations

Overview

This intake system is designed to generate a transcript from a field walk or remote session that can immediately populate a farm's grazing management site. It aligns with:

  • NRCS Grazing Plan Checklist/Worksheet — objectives + inventory + plan elements
  • Pasture Condition Scoring (PCS) — 10 indicators to benchmark pasture condition
  • Prescribed Grazing (528) — monitoring data + grazing records emphasis

The result: a working grazing dashboard from day one, with paddock maps, herd tracking, movement logs, and baseline observations.

Session Modes

🚶 In-Person Field Walk (60–90 min)

Walk the farm with the producer. Stop at key paddocks for PCS observations. Record with phone/tablet.

📹 Zoom / Remote Walk-Through (45–60 min)

Producer walks with phone camera. Share screen for map markup. Record session for transcript.

📞 Phone Intake (30–45 min)

Shorter discovery call. Follow up with map + photos. Best for initial scoping.

Transcript Tags

Encourage assessors to speak these tags aloud for easier parsing:

GOAL: Producer objectives

CONCERN: Resource concerns, risks

ACTION: Tasks, improvements planned

DECISION: Management choices

HERD: / GROUP: Livestock info

PCS: Pasture condition observations

What Gets Populated

Immediate Utility

  • Farm profile + objectives (NRCS worksheet style)
  • Paddock list with names, acres, coordinates
  • Herd & group inventory with headcounts, weights
  • Seed movement logs (last 3 moves → timeline)
  • Water system map + reliability notes
  • Fence infrastructure summary

Baseline Data

  • PCS observations by paddock (10 indicators)
  • Photo points + monitoring targets
  • Economics defaults (labor rate, hay cost)
  • Health/compliance reminders
  • Resource concern priorities
  • Action items + follow-up tasks

Conversational Question Set

Administered by grazing specialist or conservation district staff. Designed for natural conversation flow with structured data capture.

A. Opening 2–3 minutes

Goal: Permission + context + how we'll use the transcript

1 "Is it okay if I record and/or generate a transcript so we can turn it into a draft grazing plan and your farm dashboard?"
2 "What's the single most important outcome you want from today's walk/call?"
Tag: GOAL: [response]
3 "Are there any non-negotiables? Things we absolutely can't compromise on?"
  • Animal welfare requirements
  • Water quality / stream protection
  • Time constraints / labor limits
  • Budget boundaries
  • Lease restrictions
Tag: CONCERN: [response]
B. Farm Snapshot 5 minutes

Goal: Populate farm profile and basic site configuration

1 "What do you call this operation and where is it located?"
  • Farm/ranch name
  • Town, county, state
  • Tract/farm numbers (if used)
  • Total acres managed
2 "Who's involved day-to-day? What are the key roles?"
  • Primary manager/decision-maker
  • Labor help (family, hired, seasonal)
  • Who does daily moves/checks?
3 "What's your grazing season look like?"
  • Year-round vs. seasonal
  • Stockpile periods
  • Sacrifice area use
  • When do animals come off pasture?
4 "What are your primary enterprises and how do you market?"
  • Species: beef, dairy, sheep, goats, mixed
  • Marketing: direct, auction, contract, freezer beef
  • Any certifications or protocols (organic, grass-fed, etc.)
C. Objectives & Resource Concerns 7–10 minutes

Goal: Populate objectives module and align with NRCS checklists

1 "What are your top 3 objectives for the next 12 months?"

Ask open-ended first, then confirm with prompts:

  • Reduce hay feeding days?
  • Improve pasture utilization / recovery?
  • Improve water quality / streambank stability?
  • Increase profit / reduce labor?
  • Increase drought resilience?
  • Improve soil health / carbon?
  • Better animal performance / weight gains?
Tag: GOAL: [each objective]
2 "Which resource concerns are most pressing right now?"
  • Soil: erosion, compaction, organic matter
  • Water: quality, availability, reliability
  • Plants: species composition, productivity, weeds
  • Animals: health, performance, handling
  • Energy/infrastructure: fence, water system, handling
Tag: CONCERN: [each concern]
3 "What would 'success' look like by the end of the season? Can you put a number on it?"
  • Days on pasture (e.g., "extend by 30 days")
  • Stocking rate target
  • Moves per week
  • Recovery period achieved
  • Hay reduction (tons or $)
D. Livestock Inventory 8–10 minutes

Goal: Generate herds/groups/animals + performance hooks

1 "What classes of livestock do you manage as separate units?"
  • Cow/calf pairs
  • Replacement heifers
  • Stockers / backgrounders
  • Bulls / breeding groups
  • Sheep: ewes, lambs, rams
  • Goats: does, kids, bucks
  • Mixed species (flerds)
Tag: HERD: [name]
2 "For each group: headcount, average weight, and any tagged individuals?"
Tag: GROUP: [name], [herd], [count], [avg_weight]
Tag: ANIMAL: [tag], [name], [species]
3 "When do animals enter or leave the grazing platform?"
  • Calving/lambing dates
  • Weaning timing
  • When stockers arrive/ship
  • Seasonal changes to herd composition
4 "Any special management groups?"
  • Breeding groups / cleanup bulls
  • High-risk animals (thin, sick, young)
  • Animals in withdrawal
  • Guardian animals
E. Land & Paddock Inventory 10–15 minutes

Goal: Populate paddocks, acres, map layers, notes

1 "How many paddocks or management areas do you use? Let's name them and get rough acres."
Tag: PADDOCK: [name], [acres], [type]
2 "Walk me through the layout. Where are the lanes, gates, and pinch points?"
  • Main lanes / alleyways
  • Gate locations and access
  • Bottlenecks or problem spots
  • Handling facilities location
3 "Any areas with special rules or off-limits?"
  • Riparian buffers / setbacks
  • Wetlands / seasonal wet areas
  • Steep slopes
  • Sensitive habitat / wildlife areas
  • Hay ground not grazed
4 "Any leased ground or shared pastures? Different rules there?"
F. Water System Walk-Through 10 minutes

Goal: Populate water points, reliability, frost issues

1 "Where do animals drink in each major area?"
  • Troughs / tanks (capacity?)
  • Frost-free hydrants
  • Streams / creek crossings
  • Ponds / spring developments
  • Portable tanks you move
Tag: WATER: [name], [type], [paddocks], [capacity]
2 "Which water points are reliable in drought? Which ones fail first?"
3 "What fails in winter? Freeze-ups, line issues, hauling?"
  • Which lines freeze?
  • How long to thaw/fix?
  • Backup plan when frozen?
  • Labor/time cost of winter water?
4 "If you could fix ONE water bottleneck this year, what would it be?"
Tag: ACTION: water improvement — [description]
G. Fence & Handling System 8–10 minutes

Goal: Populate fence assets, efficiency bottlenecks

1 "What's your perimeter fence? Type and condition?"
2 "How do you subdivide for rotations?"
  • Polywire / reels / step-in posts
  • Electronet / netting
  • Permanent interior divisions
  • Number of paddocks you can create
Tag: FENCE: [type], [qty], [condition]
3 "What slows you down on moves?"
  • Reel tangles / broken wire
  • Gate locations inconvenient
  • Energizer issues / grounding
  • Vegetation shorting fence
4 "Any chronic 'fence down' spots or wildlife pressure?"
H. Grazing Plan Behavior 12–15 minutes

Goal: Generate movement logs, rotation metrics, monitoring approach

1 "How often do you move animals today? What triggers a move?"
  • Time-based (every X days)
  • Height-based (graze to X inches)
  • Utilization / residual target
  • Weather-driven
  • Animal behavior (restless, bawling)
2 "What's your target rest period? How does it vary by season?"
  • Spring (fast growth)
  • Summer (slower, drought)
  • Fall (stockpiling)
  • Winter (dormant)
3 "Where do you typically start the season? Where do you end?"
  • Sacrifice area strategy
  • Stockpile paddocks
  • Calving/lambing areas
4 "Tell me about your last 3 moves — when, where, which animals, and why."

⭐ This seeds immediate movement logs and timeline data

Tag: MOVE: [date], [herd], [from], [to], [reason]
5 "What's your 'can't miss' monitoring metric?"
  • Residual height
  • Ground cover %
  • Utilization estimate
  • Animal behavior / body condition
  • Species composition
I. Pasture Condition Observations (PCS) 15–25 minutes

Goal: Generate baseline without paperwork slog

PCS uses 10 indicators scored 1–5. Use conversationally:

"For each of these, tell me: where on the farm is this worst? Where is it best? Which paddocks should we score today as a benchmark?"

1. Plant Community (Desirable Species %)

"What's dominating here? Do you see diversity — cool-season grasses, legumes, warm-season?"

2. Ground Cover (%)

"Where do you see bare soil? Is it at gates, water, mineral feeders?"

3. Erosion (Rills, Gullies)

"Any rills or gullies? Sediment collecting in low spots?"

4. Compaction

"Do you see pugging or hoof shear in wet periods? Where?"

5. Manure Distribution

"Evenly spread or concentrated in certain spots?"

6. Undesirable Plants (Weeds)

"Which weeds show up repeatedly? Where are they worst?"

7. Livestock Concentration Areas

"Where do animals loaf? Shade, windbreaks, water?"

8. Residual / Utilization

"Do you have a target residual? Is it being met?"

9. Recovery (Regrowth)

"How long until regrowth is 'ready'? Spring vs. summer?"

10. Riparian / Stream Impacts

"Where are banks vulnerable? Any active erosion?"

Tag: PCS: paddock=[name] indicator=[1-10] score=[1-5] note="[observation]"
J. Economics, Labor & Risk 8–12 minutes

Goal: Populate defaults for immediate cost metrics

1 "What's your labor constraint? Minutes per day available for moves, water checks, feeding?"
2 "Do you have a labor rate for planning? Even if it's 'my time' — what's it worth per hour?"
Tag: ECON: laborRate=[$/hr]
3 "What are your key input costs?"
  • Hay cost ($/bale or $/ton)
  • Mineral program ($/head/year)
  • Fuel for moves ($/move estimate)
  • Vet/health costs ($/head/year)
Tag: ECON: hayCost=[value] mineralCost=[value]
4 "Top 3 risks this season?"
  • Drought
  • Water system failure
  • Parasite pressure
  • Infrastructure breakdown
  • Labor shortage
  • Market timing
Tag: CONCERN: risk — [description]
K. Animal Performance & Compliance 8–12 minutes

Goal: Populate health checks, reminders, protocols

1 "What's your mineral program?"
  • Type (loose, block, tub)
  • Placement strategy
  • How do you know they're consuming?
2 "What health routines are you doing? What cadence is realistic?"
  • Vaccines (annual, seasonal)
  • Foot checks / hoof trimming
  • Body condition scoring
  • Pregnancy checking
3 "Parasite management — what's your approach?"
  • Do you do FEC / FAMACHA?
  • When are parasites worst?
  • Treatment products used
  • Resistance concerns
4 "Any withdrawal requirements we should track? Treated animals for marketing?"
L. Closing 2 minutes
1 "What did we miss that would make this immediately useful next week?"
2 "Before we end, let me confirm: herd names, paddock names/acres, and your next move plan."
3 "Would you like the system to prompt follow-ups when there are gaps?"

(Default: Yes — sends SMS/email reminders for missing data)
